Catching guide

A wild miscrit's catch rate at full health gives away its stats: the better they are, the harder it is to catch. Tap the rate you see to read its rating before you spend a capture.

Catch rate by rating

Each of a miscrit's six stats rolls red, white or green, worth 0, 1 or 2 points. The total, 0 to 12, is its score. 12/12 is S+, every stat green.

Catch rate for each rating, by rarity, at full health and at 1% health

Four Exotic ratings share 1% at full health; bring it down to 1% health to tell them apart. Legendaries never roll a red stat, so none scores below C+, and their rates are only listed at 1% health.
