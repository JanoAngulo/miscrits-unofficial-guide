Breeding guide

Three miscrits go in and one of them comes back, each of its six stats copied from a parent at random. Set your three below and mark what you want to see the odds before you pay.

Work out a breed

Set each parent's stats as the game colours them. Naming a parent is optional; the same miscrit twice raises its chance of coming back.

What you want back

Miscrit
Stats

Each one must come out exactly this colour. Any leaves it open.

How breeding works

Where
Talk to Brash in the Miscrit Breeding building.
Cost
5,000 gold a breed.
Three in, one out
The result is always one of the three you put in, each with a 1 in 3 chance. Put the same miscrit in twice and it has 2 in 3.
Stats are mixed
Each of the six stats is copied from one of the three parents, picked at random. A colour two parents share has a 2 in 3 chance for that stat.
All three are used up
You lose every miscrit you put in and get only the result back. Breed miscrits you would not use anyway: a low-rated rarity you want better, and Commons with the stats you are after.

Worth the shot

A 1 in 7 chance sounds poor until you set it next to hunting. For an S+ Blighted Flowerpiller:

Hunting one in the wild

0.12% to 0.24% a catch

Breeding the A+ one you already have

14.8% a breed

Both bars on one scale, 0 to 100%.

An A+ Blighted Flowerpiller isn't a PvP pick anyway, so breeding it risks little. You won't win every breed, but the more you breed the more it pays off.
