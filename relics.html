Relic and bonus guide

Rebonus first, relic second: your bonuses decide the build. Check a roll below to see whether to stop, and which relics fit it.

Check a rebonus

    Max evolutions only; an earlier form's name finds its line. Fills in base stats and guesses the role. Change either if you know better.

    Role
    Attack
    Bonus

    In the order the game's Bonus panel lists them.

    When to stop rebonusing

    Stop once any one of these is true of your bonuses. Every miscrit has more than one good build, so stay open to what the roll gives you.

    1. A

      Lowest three add up to under 45

    2. B

      Lowest two add up to under 28

    3. C

      Lowest single stat under 8

    Your highest bonus should always land on the attack you use. Priority is attack, then ED/PD, then HP for standard miscrits, and attack 1, then ED/PD, then attack 2, then HP for hybrids.

    Which deprio to use

    With 2/6 and the deprio pity system, a good roll takes about 10 tries on average, well under 500 plats.

    The third stat you give up is usually HP. There are exceptions, but that is the general rule.

    Relic builds

    Numbered as the guide numbers them. Only go slow on a 1/5 speed miscrit with under 7 speed bonus; otherwise a standard bruiser or tank is worth more.

    Choosing between builds

    • Lots of true damage

      Lean toward a defensive tank, builds 5 to 8.

    • Lots of raw damage or healing

      Lean toward a bruiser stat check, builds 1 to 4.

    • Snipers

      Only relic a sniper you stopped on B or C. Bruiser (build 10) is always safe. Some can run the speed build (9), depending on the meta; if in doubt, build 10.

    • Temple Stone or Magicite Staff, Vanquished Soul or Dark Warro Thorn

      For builds 10 and 11: a miscrit with 3/5 or better defenses and more than 25 bonus gets more duel value from Temple Stone or Vanquished Soul.

    • Bauble or Gold Piece / White Gem, for hybrids

      If the miscrit already heals or blocks, Gold Piece is usually better. If it stacks stats or has lower HP, Bauble is better.
